# Course alignment

Source: *Explore the Responsible Use of Generative AI in Academic Work — Lecturers Kick-off*, September 2026. The source deck is kept outside this repository.

## Audience and tools

- The kickoff reports 33 of 40 places registered, across ETH Zurich, the University of Zurich, and the University of Basel, with participants from more than 14 PhD programmes or tracks.
- The participant survey has 27 responses. Thirteen respondents had never seen Python code on their computer, while 24 use language models daily or several times a week. Use copy-paste prompts for installation.
- The course plans ChatGPT as a common baseline for one month. Gemini and Claude access is uneven. Examples should not rely on a single university account or an extra paid service.
- The survey lists literature review, AI agents, and verification among student interests. Eighteen respondents named verifying outputs as a barrier; 14 named data privacy concerns.

## Workshop fit

The Research Assistants session is scheduled for 30 September 2026, 10:45–13:00, at ETH HG F26.3. The kickoff describes four scientific use cases for this session: literature search, research-paper summaries, code for data analysis, and simple web applications for presenting results.

Prepare a subscription article and an openly accessible alternative. Have students check the citation and open the saved PDF in Zotero. Keep restricted PDFs and student data out of this Git repository.
